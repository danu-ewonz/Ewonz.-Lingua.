📘 About EWONZ LINGUA

EWONZ LINGUA is a smart language learning app designed to help students learn Japanese in a simple and interactive way.

This app provides structured lessons, vocabulary lists, grammar guides, and reading practice to make learning easier for beginners to advanced learners.

Our goal is to make language learning accessible, fun, and effective for everyone in Sri Lanka and beyond.

⭐ Features

• Step-by-step learning system
• Beginner to Intermediate roadmap
• Vocabulary & grammar sections
• Easy navigation with sidebar system
